URN Audio Channel
=================

PURPOSE
-------
Visual stereo channel utility for adjusting left/right level, swapping channels, static panning, or moving a sound across the stereo field over the full clip duration.

INPUTS / SETTINGS
-----------------
audio_input
- Required AUDIO input.
- Mono audio is automatically converted to stereo so panning can work.

Left Volume
- Left-channel level from 0-200%.
- 100% = unchanged.
- Below 100% reduces the left channel; above 100% boosts it.

Right Volume
- Right-channel level from 0-200%.
- 100% = unchanged.

Swap L / R
- Off: leave channels in their original positions.
- On: exchange the left and right channels.

Pan
- Used when Auto Pan = Off.
- -100 = fully left.
- 0 = centre.
- +100 = fully right.
- The large Stereo Position handle provides the visual control for this setting.

Auto Pan
- Off: use the static Pan position.
- Left -> Right: sound moves from full left to full right over the complete input duration.
- Right -> Left: sound moves from full right to full left over the complete input duration.

Movement Curve / Pan Curve
- Cosine: smooth acceleration/deceleration through the stereo field; recommended for natural fly-by movement.
- Linear: position changes at a constant rate from one side to the other.

Prevent Clipping
- On: if the processed result would exceed the safe digital peak range, the whole result is reduced just enough to avoid clipping.
- Off: keep the requested gains exactly, even if clipping occurs.

Reset
- Restores the visual controls to their default values.

OUTPUTS
-------
audio
- Processed stereo AUDIO.

EXAMPLE
-------
For a plane passing from left to right:
- Auto Pan = Left -> Right
- Pan Curve = Cosine
- Leave Left/Right Volume at 100% unless level correction is also needed.
