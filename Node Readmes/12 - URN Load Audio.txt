URN Load Audio
==============

Loads an audio file from ComfyUI input and outputs:
- AUDIO
- Embedded_Lyrics
- Artist
- Title
- Album
- Source_Filename
- Metadata_JSON

Embedded lyrics created by URN Save Audio with Lyrics are recognised from:
- MP3: ID3 USLT
- FLAC: Vorbis Comment LYRICS

Artist/title metadata can be connected to URN Audio Lyrics so online lookup does not need to guess the identity from the filename.
